Delete your Painlivo data

Painlivo does not require account registration. Your pain and symptom entries are stored locally on your device in encrypted form, not uploaded to a Painlivo server.

How to delete local data

  1. Open Painlivo on your device.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete My Data.
  3. Review the confirmation and confirm deletion.

What is deleted?

The in-app deletion action removes locally stored data covered by that feature. Verify the precise scope in the installed app. Backups, exported PDF reports and files that you saved or shared outside Painlivo must be removed separately from their respective locations.

Third-party services

Deleting local data does not automatically delete transaction records or technical and advertising data processed independently by Google Play, Google AdMob or RevenueCat. Their retention and deletion rules are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Questions or deletion assistance

If you no longer have access to the app or need help understanding what can be deleted, contact us. Because Painlivo has no user account or server-side pain diary, we cannot remotely delete locally stored records from your device. Do not send sensitive health data unless necessary.
