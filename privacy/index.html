Painlivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Painlivo is a pain and symptom tracking app. For privacy inquiries, contact zindemedya@gmail.com.

Health records

The app is designed to store the pain locations, intensity, possible triggers, notes and tracking history you enter locally on your device. You do not need to create an account. These records are not uploaded to a Painlivo-operated account server.

Backup and export

If you choose to export or email a backup, the backup file is generated by the app and is intended to be encrypted. Any file you choose to send or store through an email, cloud storage or sharing provider is handled under that provider's terms and privacy practices. You control the recipient and destination. Avoid sending your backup to an unintended recipient.

Advertising

The free version may display banner ads and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address and technical information, depending on platform settings, consent choices and applicable law. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy. Ad personalization and consent options are determined by the implementation and applicable local requirements.

In-app purchases

You may purchase an option to remove ads. Purchases and their restoration are handled through the relevant app store (Google Play or Apple App Store), which processes the transaction under its own privacy policy. Painlivo does not need to receive your payment card details.

Permissions and notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app may request notification permission. Backups, exports or importing files may require access through the device's file picker or sharing tools. You can manage permissions in device settings.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them using available app controls or remove the app's local data. Copies you exported or emailed must be deleted separately wherever they are stored. Deleting the app does not automatically remove emailed or cloud-stored backup copies.

Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to enter health records, export backups, enable reminders or make a purchase. For privacy-related questions, contact zindemedya@gmail.com.

Medical notice

Painlivo is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional healthcare advice.
