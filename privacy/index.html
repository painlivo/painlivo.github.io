Painlivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Painlivo is a pain and symptom tracking app. For privacy inquiries, contact .

Health records

Painlivo stores the pain locations, intensity, possible triggers, notes and tracking history you enter locally on your device. You do not need an account. Painlivo does not upload these records to its own server.

Backup and export

If you choose to export or email a backup, the app generates a backup file intended to be encrypted. Files you choose to send or store through email, cloud storage or sharing services are handled under those providers’ privacy practices. You control the recipient and destination. Check the recipient before sharing health data.

Advertising

The free version may display banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Depending on platform settings and consent, advertising providers may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address and technical information. See Google’s Privacy Policy for more information. Ad consent options depend on the app implementation and applicable law. Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

You can purchase an option to remove ads. Payments and purchase restoration are handled by Google Play or Apple App Store under their respective privacy policies. Painlivo does not require your payment card details.

Permissions and reminders

If you enable daily reminders, Painlivo may request notification permission. Exporting or importing backups may use your device’s file picker or sharing tools. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

Storage and deletion

Records stored locally remain until you delete them using app features or delete the app’s local data. You must separately delete exported or emailed backups from the places where you stored them. Uninstalling the app does not delete copies stored elsewhere.

Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to enter records, export backups, enable reminders, view ads or remove ads with a purchase. For privacy questions, use the contact button below.

Medical notice

Painlivo is for personal tracking only. It does not diagnose medical conditions and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
